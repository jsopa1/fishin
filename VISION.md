# Fishing Forecast — Vision

## Mission

Build an evidence-based fishing intelligence product that helps anglers make better decisions about when, where, and how to fish.

## Initial Product

A fishing forecast that combines environmental conditions, seasonality, location, species, and eventually real fishing observations to estimate fishing conditions.

## Long-Term Vision

Help anglers answer:

* Should I fish today?
* When should I go?
* Where should I go?
* What species should I target?
* What should I use?
* Why does the system think that?
* How confident is the prediction?

## Product Philosophy

We value usefulness over novelty, evidence over speculation, prediction over generic advice, transparency over false confidence, and simple products over unnecessary complexity.

## Engineering Philosophy

* Prefer simple solutions.
* Validate assumptions.
* Measure before claiming success.
* Automate repetitive work.
* Keep humans responsible for important decisions.
* Let agents execute well-defined work.
* Document important decisions.
* Optimize for learning and correctness rather than scale.

## Business Philosophy

This is primarily an engineering, product, and learning project. We are not initially optimizing for revenue, growth, fundraising, or startup economics. Initial budget: $0.

## Agent Philosophy

Agents should understand the vision and current priorities, propose and execute assigned work, document findings and blockers, challenge bad assumptions, and ask the CEO for necessary decisions. The CEO should not manually orchestrate routine work.
