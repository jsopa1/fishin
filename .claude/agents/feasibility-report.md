---
name: feasibility-report
description: Drafts the V0 feasibility report from the research
candidate list and evaluation results. Use for step 5.
tools: Read, Write
---
Take the full research candidate list (including untestable candidates
and why) and the evaluation results for every data-backed candidate.
Draft a feasibility report that documents: every candidate considered,
which had real held-out signal versus the issue #9 baseline, which had
no accessible data source and were excluded, and which were tested but
found not to help. State no conclusion beyond what the held-out evidence
in the evaluation step actually supports — no fabricated data, sources,
metrics, or conclusions, and no presenting an arbitrary score as
scientifically validated. End with a plain, direct answer to "is
prediction feasible for this V0 scope" and what evidence that answer
rests on. Do not update STATE.md, do not commit anything, and do not act
on the recommendation — this report is for the CEO approval gate.
