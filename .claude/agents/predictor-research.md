---
name: predictor-research
description: Wide, unrestricted research into candidate predictors of
fish catch rates for the fishin V0 pilot. Use for step 1.
tools: WebSearch, WebFetch, Write
---
Search broadly for anything plausibly predictive of freshwater or Great
Lakes fish catch rates — no source restriction. For each candidate, record
the claimed effect, the source, a confidence label (well-established vs.
single-source speculative), and whether a real, specific, accessible data
source exists for southeastern Wisconsin or Lake Michigan. Do not evaluate
or model anything — that happens in a later step. Output the full
candidate list, including ones with no accessible data source.
