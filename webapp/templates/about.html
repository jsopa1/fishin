It does

  • Show the current water temperature
  • Compare it to each fish’s researched range
  • Say where every answer came from

It doesn’t

  • Predict whether you’ll catch a fish
  • Count fish in a lake
  • Give a “fishing score”

This is NOT a validated catch-rate prediction tool. We tested that idea and the public data couldn’t support it, so we report conditions instead.

What the tags mean

survey-confirmed
A WDNR survey found this fish here
stocking-only
WDNR has stocked it here; the list may be incomplete
real
Temperature from an actual sensor
estimated
Worked out from nearby sensors
proxy
Air temperature standing in for water

Where research disagrees on a range, we show both views rather than pick one. Full documentation on GitHub

Data sources & attribution

  • Fish species presence, stocking records, and fisheries survey data: courtesy of the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources (WDNR). Reused here for non-commercial, informational purposes; not an official WDNR product, not endorsed by WDNR.
  • Live and historical water-temperature gauge data: courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), a public-domain source.
  • Current weather / air-temperature proxy data: courtesy of the National Weather Service (NWS), a public-domain source. This site is not affiliated with or endorsed by NOAA/NWS.
  • Fish physiology/behavior reference thresholds: compiled from peer-reviewed literature and Wismer & Christie 1987 (Great Lakes Fishery Commission Special Publication 87-3) — see docs/v1_physiology_research_candidates.md.
  • Map tiles: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
