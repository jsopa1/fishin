Privacy Policy

This is a drafted starting point, written by describing exactly what this app's code actually does — it is not a substitute for review by a qualified professional before this app is relied on commercially.

Last updated 16 September 2026.

The short version

There are no accounts and no login. Every core feature of this app — browsing waterbodies, checking conditions, viewing a spot report, and saving spots — works with nothing sent to a server beyond the page requests needed to show you the page.

Saved spots

The "Save this spot" button on a spot page stores your saved spots in your browser's localStorage only. This data is never sent to our server, never included in any request we make, and never visible to us in any form. Clearing your browser's site data for this app deletes it permanently, and we have no copy.

Preferences and location

The Profile page stores your theme, the kinds of spots you prefer or avoid, how far you will travel and the fish you target in your browser's localStorage only. None of it is sent to our server or seen by us. If you use your device's location to sort spots by distance, it is used on your own device and is never sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes all of it.

Feedback

If you use the feedback form, we store the message you write, the page you were on, and an email address only if you choose to include one. There's no account to link it to — it's always anonymous unless you choose to leave contact info.

Anonymous analytics

We log page views and spot-saves against a random token stored in a first-party session cookie — not shared with or sold to anyone. This lets us see roughly how many pages someone visits and whether they return, without knowing who they are. We never record an IP address or browser/device identifying string in this data, by design — an automated test in this project's own test suite fails if that ever changes without a deliberate decision to do so.

Live third-party data

Every time you view a spot or waterbody page, the app fetches live data on your behalf from the Wisconsin DNR, USGS, NOAA, and the National Weather Service — water temperature, regulations, consumption advisories, and current weather. These are server-to-server calls; your personal information is never included in them.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no passwords, nothing to log in with.
  • No third-party analytics, ad networks, or tracking pixels of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No cookies beyond the one first-party session cookie described above.

Questions

Use the feedback form for any privacy question.
