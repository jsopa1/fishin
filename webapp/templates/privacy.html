Privacy Policy

This is a drafted starting point, written by describing exactly what this app's code actually does — it is not a substitute for review by a qualified professional before this app is relied on commercially.

Last updated 15 September 2026.

The short version

You can use every core feature of this app — browsing waterbodies, checking conditions, viewing a spot report, and saving spots — with no account and nothing sent to a server beyond the page requests needed to show you the page. Creating an account is optional, and only needed if you want to log catches and have them follow you across devices.

Saved spots (no account)

The "Save this spot" button on a spot page stores your saved spots in your browser's localStorage only. This data is never sent to our server, never included in any request we make, and never visible to us in any form. Clearing your browser's site data for this app deletes it permanently, and we have no copy.

If you create an account

We store:

  • Your email address and a hashed password (we never store or can recover your actual password — see below).
  • Catches you log: species, the coordinate and spot name of where you logged it, the date, any notes you write, and whether you kept or released the fish.

Passwords are hashed with a standard, one-way algorithm (Werkzeug's password hashing, built on PBKDF2) before being stored — we cannot look up or recover your password, only verify a login attempt against the hash.

Feedback

If you use the feedback form, we store the message you write, the page you were on, and an email address only if you choose to include one. If you're logged in, your submission is linked to your account so we can follow up; if not, it's anonymous.

Anonymous analytics

We log page views and spot-saves against a random token stored in the same session cookie used to keep you logged in — not a separate tracking cookie, and not shared with or sold to anyone. This lets us see roughly how many pages someone visits and whether they return, without knowing who they are. We never record an IP address or browser/device identifying string in this data, by design — an automated test in this project's own test suite fails if that ever changes without a deliberate decision to do so.

Live third-party data

Every time you view a spot or waterbody page, the app fetches live data on your behalf from the Wisconsin DNR, USGS, NOAA, and the National Weather Service — water temperature, regulations, consumption advisories, and current weather. These are server-to-server calls; your personal information is never included in them.

What we don't do

  • No third-party analytics, ad networks, or tracking pixels of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No cookies beyond the one first-party session cookie described above.

Deleting your data

We don't yet have a self-service delete-my-account flow. Contact us through the feedback form and include the email your account uses, and we'll delete your account, your logged catches, and any feedback tied to your account.

Questions

Use the feedback form for any privacy question.
