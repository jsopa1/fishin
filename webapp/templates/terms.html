What this is

fishin is an informational tool that reports real, current water conditions compared against published fish-physiology research, for species documented as present in a given water. Full detail on what it is and — just as importantly — what it is not is on the About page: it is not a validated catch-rate prediction, it does not estimate how many fish are in a water, and nothing it says is a guarantee you will catch anything.

Use at your own judgment

Every reading and every statement here carries its own source and evidentiary tier, shown in full — real, estimated, proxy, survey-confirmed, stocking-only. Treat these as inputs to your own judgment, not as a substitute for it. Regulations and consumption advisories are shown from WDNR's own live service with a retrieval time, but you are responsible for verifying current rules directly with WDNR before keeping any fish — a page here reflecting a cached or delayed reading is possible, and rules change.

No warranty

This service is provided "as is." Water temperature, species, and regulation data ultimately come from third-party government sources (WDNR, USGS, NOAA, NWS) that can be delayed, wrong, or temporarily unavailable, and this app's own interpolation and estimates carry explicitly labeled uncertainty. We make no guarantee the service will be available at any given time, or that any reading is current or error-free.
