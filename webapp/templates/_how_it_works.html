How it works

  1. 1

    Read the water

    We take the current temperature from real Wisconsin gauges, or the closest estimate when a lake has none.

  3. 2

    Check the research

    Published studies give each fish a temperature range where it is most active and feeding.

  5. 3

    See what’s in range

    Fish WDNR records show at that spot, and whose range matches the water right now, are listed.

“In range” is not “biting.” It can’t tell you whether you’ll catch one.
